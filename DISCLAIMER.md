# Independence, provenance and responsible use

**Independent educational case study. Not affiliated with, commissioned, sponsored, endorsed or approved by Henkel.** No internal employee access or confidential financial, CRM, distributor, customer, order, pricing or experiment data were used. Do not present the project as an internal company study or portray simulated cases as actual Henkel operations.

## Evidence tiers
1. `data/official_financials/`: manually transcribed public financial metrics, with reporting periods, disclosure statuses and official PDF links. Refer to Henkel's original statement when quoting numbers.
2. `data/desk_research/`: publicly documented market/manufacturer/product observations, plus clearly indicated hypotheses and unknowns. The existence of a manufacturer is **not** an actual Henkel prospect, customer, win/loss, relationship or market share claim.
3. `data/assumption_models/`: hypothetical market and unit-economic inputs. Not forecasts.
4. `data/synthetic/`: invented customer/distributor/account IDs, quantities, pricing, costs, trials, conversion and experiment outcomes. These are **not real Henkel business results**.

Do not join simulated sales to official actual financial results as if sourced from one corporate system. The original 1,440 simulated distributor observations are preserved as a CSV export and in the final workbook.

## Methodological limits
- Organic growth is a reported company-defined measure; price/volume terms are percentage-point contributions, not automatically a simple multiplication of unit price and quantities.
- Adhesive Technologies business-unit growth and APC business-area growth cannot be mixed; APC-specific price/volume and margin numbers may be undisclosed.
- Distributor diagnostics are descriptive and simulated. Root-cause labels are hypotheses until verified.
- Synthetic pre/post differences and bootstrap intervals do not prove actual treatment effects; no live commercial experiment has been conducted.
- No real-world pilot, contact, targeting or pricing intervention is recommended without appropriate approvals, privacy review, product safety and competition compliance.
